# Thinking With Minions 🧠🍌

Welcome to **Thinking With Minions** — a magazine-style knowledge hub exploring the thoughtful, practical, and culture-shifting use of large language models (LLMs) in business.

This repository contains a series of articles written for curious leaders, creative teams, and forward-thinking builders who want to understand — and guide — the rise of minion-like assistants in their workflows.

## 🔍 What's Inside

The magazine works in three layers:
- **The main site** introduces the concepts in short, plain-language articles.
- **[IT, Tech and Developer Topics](https://glcapps.github.io/ThinkingWithMinions/developers/)** is where IT and engineering readers go next.
- **[Substack](https://glcapps.substack.com/)** carries the deeper dives. Many articles end with a link to the full version.

### 🧭 Core Ideas
- **The Minion Ethos** — Why thinking *with* LLMs beats treating them like magic
- **How to Brief a Minion** — Structure and intent in every request
- **The Anatomy of a Prompt** — Clarify the task, supply facts, set tone
- **Context Applications** — Build the environment the model works inside
- **Not Everything Is a Minion Task** — Knowing when *not* to delegate

- **The Hidden Constraint in LLM Systems** — A large window is not a large working surface

- **The Post-Webpage Economy** — When the page stops being the unit of encounter
- **Beyond Endpoints: The Rise of Data Landscapes** — From payloads to navigable meaning
- **Avoiding the RAG Trap** and **Don't Just RAG** — When explicit context beats retrieval
- **Minion Integration Is About Workflow, Not Hype** — Bounded delegation inside real workflows
- **The New Spreadsheet Moment** — What spreadsheets did for numbers, LLMs do for language

### 🏢 Business & Governance

### 📓 Working With Minions
